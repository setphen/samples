
LEGOWELT SEQUENTIAL CIRCUITS INC. PROPHET 600 SYNTHESIZER SAMPLE PACK

386 wav samples in 16 bit 44khz mono artisanally handmade on the Prophet 600
Synthesizer in Legowelts studio

Mostly one shot bass sounds, a lot of synth chords, washy pads and strings also
A lot of bleepy FX

Please note the root keys are all over the place (due to the Prophet 600's vaguely erratic frequency knob system) so some samples might not sampled in a C note - but 
who cares, its not like we are going to play these samples like a grand piano...just play it by ear ;)

The Sequential Circuits inc. Prophet 600 is a polyphonic analog synthesizer from 1982 

This is a particular good synthesizer for (electro) bass sounds - it might even 
surpass the legendary Pro one as unlike that instrument the Prophet 600's sounds retain a lot of their bass frequency When the resonance goes up. Definitely not as 
'fluid' as a pro one but the Prophet 600 has a lot more 'power' in its sound.

The prophet 600 in Legowelt's studio is serial number 422 it hasn't been updated with the Gibli mod yet but has an uprgraded Fatar keyboard which plays really nice.

Anyways, these samples are free to use in your music productions load them in your
